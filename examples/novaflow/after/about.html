We kept solving the same incident twice

NovaFlow started at a payments company where the answer to most 2am pages already existed, scattered across a Slack thread from three months earlier. Finding it took longer than fixing the problem.

We are a team of three engineers who have all carried a pager. We build NovaFlow for teams of five to fifty people who write things down in chat and want that writing to be usable the next time.

We answer support email ourselves. Write to hello@novaflow.dev and you will hear back from one of us, usually the same day.

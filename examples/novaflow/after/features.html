What NovaFlow keeps, and what it leaves in the thread

A good runbook is shorter than the conversation that produced it. These are the rules NovaFlow follows when it drafts one.

Kept

Commands and links
Shell commands, dashboard links, and file paths, in the order they were used, with the message they came from.
Decisions
Messages like "use the restricted key, not the full one" become notes on the step they affect.
Owners
The person who ran each step is suggested as its owner. You confirm or change it before saving.

Left out

Dead ends
Attempts that were undone later in the thread are shown to you, unticked, so you can decide whether they belong.
Secrets
Anything that looks like a token or password is replaced with the name of the variable that should hold it.

Keeping runbooks current

When someone runs a runbook and does something differently, NovaFlow asks at the end whether the change should become the new version. Old versions stay in the history.

Privacy

NovaFlow stores the text of threads you send it, the runbooks made from them, and who ran which step. We do not read other channels and we do not sell or share your data.

To delete your workspace and everything in it, use the settings page or email hello@novaflow.dev. Deletion completes within 30 days.
